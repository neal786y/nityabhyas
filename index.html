Why it works

Hindi, English and offline

Questions

Frequently asked

Are these real exam questions?

No. Questions are created from checked patterns that follow each exam's published syllabus and style, so they are fresh every day. Nityabhyas doesn't use past papers and isn't affiliated with any exam body.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Practice, tests, cards and results all work offline. You need internet only to buy or restore Pro.

Can I use it in Hindi?

Yes. The whole app, every question and explanation, works in Hindi and English. You can switch any time, even in the middle of a test.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Nityabhyas Pro, and tap Cancel. Pro stays until the end of the month you paid for.

What data do you collect?

Your practice stays on your phone. The app sends anonymous usage and crash data (no name, email or advertising ID) to improve it; turn it off any time in Settings. Read the privacy policy.
