Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Developer: Nilesh Yadav, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India · Contact: creativitysyncing@gmail.com

Nityabhyas ("the app") is an aptitude and exam-practice app. It is built to work offline, and it does not ask you to create an account. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is meant for people aged 18 and over.

We do not: show ads, use the advertising ID, sell data, or track you across other apps or websites. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or UPI details.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: practice data stays until you reset it or uninstall the app. The app keeps detailed answers for 365 days and then keeps only daily totals.
  • Usage data: kept by Firebase Analytics for up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Question reports: kept as long as they help keep questions correct. They don't identify you.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as needed for billing and legal requirements.

Your rights and choices

  • Turn off usage data: Settings → "Share anonymous usage data". It stops at once, and data waiting on the phone is discarded.
  • Delete your practice data: Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the app.
  • Ask about or delete data held by our service providers: write to creativitysyncing@gmail.com. Because the data is anonymous, tell us roughly when you used the app and on which phone model so we can try to find it.
  • Grievance officer (DPDP Act, 2023): Nilesh Yadav, creativitysyncing@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Security

Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox; it isn't separately encrypted, because it holds only your practice history, settings and an optional first name. Data sent to our service providers travels over encrypted connections. Question reports can only be written by the genuine app, verified through Google Play Integrity.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
