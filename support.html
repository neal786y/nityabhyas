Help and support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Write to us

support@nityabhyas.in

Mention your app version (Settings → Help, privacy & terms shows it). We reply within a few working days. Never send passwords or payment details.

Questions

Are these real exam questions?

No. Every question is original and follows the pattern of the exam you chose. They aren't official or past papers.

How does the free daily limit work?

Free practice is 15 questions a day at Easy and Medium levels, plus 3 Speed Gym drills and 20 GK cards. It resets at midnight on your phone.

What does Pro include?

Unlimited practice at every level, shortcut methods, unlimited mock and sectional tests, unlimited Speed Gym and cards, full history and your readiness score.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Nityabhyas Pro → Cancel. Pro stays until the end of the month you paid for.

I changed phones. Is my progress saved?

Android's backup restores your progress when you sign in with the same Google account. Pro comes back with Settings → Restore purchase.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Practice, tests, cards and progress all work offline. Only buying or restoring Pro needs the internet.

What is a streak freeze?

If you miss a day, a freeze keeps your streak going. You get 1 freeze a week (2 with Pro), and it's used automatically.

I found a wrong question. What should I do?

Tap the flag on the question and choose what is wrong. We check every report and fix or withdraw the question.
